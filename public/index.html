Resilience Architecture & Speculative Hedging

⚡

Zero-Latency Circuit Short-Circuit

When an upstream provider's failures hit the threshold (3), the circuit transitions to OPEN. Further requests immediately skip the dead provider without waiting for network timeouts, instantly cascading to healthy fallbacks.

🎯

Canary Probing (HALF-OPEN)

After exponential cooldown passes ($2^{\text{trips}} \times 10\text{s}$), the circuit enters HALF-OPEN, cautiously allowing probe requests. If 2 consecutive probes succeed, the circuit closes. If any probe fails, it re-trips immediately.

🏎️

Request Hedging (Speculative Backup)

Portkey & Envoy style speculative dispatch: if the primary provider stalls past the delay threshold (e.g. 2000ms), Gateway fires the backup provider in parallel. The fastest winner is returned, and the loser is aborted cleanly.
